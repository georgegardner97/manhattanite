# Manhattanite — Decision Inventory v1

Prepared 2026-08-31 for the Miro decision matrix ("one decision per day").

Every open call on the record, plus the ones nobody has written down yet.
**†** marks a decision that has never been raised before in the project files.

Eight of these block the first invitations (wave one, 1–13 Sep). They are Group A.
Everything else can wait its turn.

---

## A. Blocks the first invitations (next two weeks)

1. **Example listings** — leave them up, relabel them as examples, or clear them before real members arrive.
2. **Listing mix** — 12 of the 20 listings are apartments. Write non-apartment listings, or accept that the site reads as a rental portal.
3. **The first ten names** — pick them from the 38-name list.
4. **The one-line pitch** — options were drafted 1 Aug and never chosen. The invitation can't be written until this is.
5. **How an invitation physically reaches someone** † — an in-product invite link (built, but nothing links to it), or a personal email with a signup link.
6. **Founder-authored listings** † — do the seed listings stay under your name once real members are looking at them.
7. **A report button** † — there is no way to report a listing. Build one before strangers arrive, or accept email-only.
8. **Support address** — hello@ vs info@, who answers it, and how fast.

## B. Money

9. **Charge or don't** — $99 for a 30-day apartment listing.
10. **Furniture free forever** — confirm or reverse.
11. **Free-until-rented, or a strict 30 days.**
12. **What switches paid on** — a member count, or observed repeat posting.
13. **Jobs at $75** in v1.5 — in or out.
14. **Whether the audience is ever sold** — ads, data, broker packages. Currently ruled out; worth a dated re-read rather than a standing assumption.

## C. Legal and entity

15. **Form the entity now or hold** — NY LLC vs Delaware C-corp.
16. **Engage an attorney** — budget, and when.
17. **Fair-housing specialist review** before any apartment listing that isn't yours.
18. **Attorney review of Terms and Privacy** — timing.
19. **Fair housing in the product** † — an attestation checkbox on the apartment form, or keep it a manual check at moderation.
20. **Trademark the name.**
21. **Insurance.**
22. **Sales tax on listing fees** — a specific question to ask counsel.
23. **Data deletion requests** — the actual process when someone asks.

## D. The trust mechanic

24. **Vouch floor** — stay at one voucher, or move to two, and when.
25. **Can a new member vouch immediately** †, or only after a period inside.
26. **A cap on how many people one member may vouch for** † per month.
27. **What happens to the people someone vouched for when that member is removed** † — cascade, or leave them standing.
28. **Keep the open apply path after seed**, or go vouch-only.
29. **Who arbitrates a dispute between two members** — and whether Manhattanite says anything at all.
30. **Off-platform behaviour reports** — how they are handled.
31. **Identity verification** — real name plus photo is the whole of it today. Enough or not.
32. **A second reviewer** † — recruit a co-admin, or stay a one-person queue including the weekends you're away.
33. **The graded trust score** (Explorer / Verified / Trusted / Connector) — keep it as the v2 direction or drop it.

## E. Product scope

34. **Do listings expire** † — 30-day auto-expiry, or they stay up until someone removes them.
35. **A weekly digest email** † — currently there is no reason for a member to come back. Build one or don't.
36. **Wanted / "looking for" listings** (parked 27 Aug).
37. **Price modes** — free / make an offer / rate on request (parked 27 Aug).
38. **New categories** — jobs, services, discounts, and whether "any right listing" is genuinely allowed.
39. **Sublets** † — own category or folded into apartments.
40. **Geography** † — Manhattan only, or Brooklyn and beyond. The name says one thing; supply may say another.
41. **In-product messaging** — what event moves it out of v2 and into the build.
42. **Photo weight** — resize on upload (cheapest), pay Supabase, or leave it.
43. **Analytics** — turn Plausible on (~$9/mo) or stay blind.
44. **Email confirmation at signup** — currently off; Turnstile holds the door alone.

## F. Brand and design

46. **The frozen calls** — second typeface, accent colour.
47. **A licensed hero photograph** — buy one or don't.
48. **Profile photos** — ship the avatar work or leave it.
49. **Five content widths** — collapse them to a scale.
50. **Instagram, and printed cards** — presence or not.

## G. Founder and direction

52. **The audience** — older and wealthier, or younger and culturally driven. The oldest open one, held since 13 Aug.
53. **The three-pillar framing** (discounts / vetted services / trusted buy-sell) — positioning language, or a real category roadmap.
54. **Cole** — formalize the partnership, and on what basis.
55. **Paid acquisition** — the bar a channel would have to clear.
56. **The two-day founder routine** — keep the cadence or change it.
57. **Ambition** — lifestyle business, or something you raise money for.
58. **Bus factor** † — the admin key lives on your laptop and nowhere else. Decide a backup.

---

## A note on the matrix axes

The Miro template's second axis is "likelihood of success", which is built for actions, not decisions. For a list like this it reads better as **"how much does answering it change what happens next"**. Difficulty stays as it is. Otherwise most of these land in the same corner and the board stops sorting anything.
