# Contacts

People George interacts with. Updated by the PA when someone is mentioned by name with context. Used for: knowing who's who in a briefing, threading prior interactions into meeting prep, flagging overdue replies to important contacts.

Format:

```
## Full Name
- **Role/relationship:** what they do, how they know George
- **Channels:** Outlook | Gmail | other
- **Reply priority:** important | warm | cold
- **Last interaction:** YYYY-MM-DD — short note
- **Open threads:** anything waiting on George or vice versa
```

**Priority tiers (drive overdue-reply surfacing):**
- **Important** — reply within 3 days. Investors, advisors, partners, anyone whose response unblocks the build.
- **Warm** — reply within 7 days. Friends, peripheral contacts, light commercial.
- **Cold** — reply when it fits. Cold inbound, newsletters, etc.
