Why it exists

The good listings never make it to the open web.

They move through group chats. A friend of a friend. A text to the three people who'd actually know. The signal was always in the network — we just built the marketplace around it, instead of around strangers.

How it works

Trust, built in — not patched on.

01

Everyone's been vouched for.

Each member is someone's referral, and their sponsor is named in the open. Nobody enters the network anonymously.

02

Every listing passes a person.

Nothing goes public automatically. Each one is read and approved first. The bar is taste, not a list of rules.

03

Bad actors don't get policed.

They don't get in. Quality compounds because the network polices itself — every cohort raises the bar for the next.
