## 2. The promise

[Three short blocks side-by-side on desktop, stacked on mobile. Each is a complete thought, not a marketing bullet. No icons. Sentence-case headings.]

### Better stuff
Every listing is read by a person before it goes live. No bait, no broker spam, no Craigslist energy. You see what's actually worth seeing.

### Trust the people
Members are sponsored by name. The person who brought them in shows up on their profile. If someone behaves badly, their sponsor knows about it.

### You're in, or you're not
We don't have a free trial of belonging. Anyone can make an account and look around. Posting, messaging, and sponsoring are for members.

---

## 3. How it works

[A quiet, editorial section. Two columns or one — designer's call. No diagrams. Voice carries it.]

### Two tiers, one network.

**Account.** Free. Anyone with an email. Browse every listing, see who posted it, read profiles. No application, no review, no paywall.

**Member.** By application, or by invite from an existing member. Required to post a listing, message a member, or sponsor someone new. Your sponsor's name appears on your profile — publicly, permanently.

That's the whole model. Account holders see the network. Members make it move.

## 5. On sponsorship

[Single editorial paragraph block. No CTAs in this section — it earns trust, doesn't sell.]

Every member is brought in by another member. There are no anonymous members on Manhattanite, and no anonymous sponsorships. Your sponsor's name sits on your profile next to yours. If you'd want to live next door to someone, sponsor them. If you wouldn't, don't.

During the seed phase, new members are sponsored by the founder. As the network grows, sponsorship moves to existing members.

## Notes for the build

- **No popups.** No exit intent, no email capture overlay, no "limited spots" banner. The brand guide explicitly bans urgency theatre.
- **No stock photography.** If there's an image at all on v2, it's an editorial detail shot — a door buzzer, a brownstone stoop, a handwritten label. Brand guide section "Photography and imagery."
- **One CTA pair, repeated.** "Apply for membership" / "I have an invite." Don't introduce a third CTA verb.
- **American spelling.** "Neighborhood", "favorite", "color." Even though the founder's personal voice runs British, Manhattanite is American. See `COMPANY/voice-and-copy.md`.
- **One exclamation mark across the whole page, maximum.** Currently zero. Keep it that way.

---

## Five-point test (per writing-rules.md)

1. **Specificity.** Real categories named (apartments, furniture). Real neighborhoods (West Village, East Village, LES, UWS). Real mechanic (sponsor named on profile). ✓
2. **Necessity.** Every section earns its place: hero (what it is), promise (why), how it works (mechanic), what's listed (proof), sponsorship (trust math), founding cohort (honesty), footer. No section is decoration. ✓
3. **Voice.** Sounds like Manhattanite — Soho House cadence, editorial paragraphs, no buzzwords. Could not be Craigslist, Nextdoor, or Facebook Marketplace. ✓
4. **Action.** Two CTAs, repeated. Clear: apply, or use an invite. No buried next step. ✓
5. **Honesty.** No overclaim. "The product is real and working" is true post-Phase 1. "Membership is small and growing slowly" is true. We do not claim curation we haven't done. ✓
