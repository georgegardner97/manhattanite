## Five-point test (per writing-rules.md)

1. **Specificity.** Real streets (Bank, Greene, East 78th, Orchard, Vandam, East 88th), real brands (Ceccotti, BDDW, Knoll, Carl Hansen, Flos, Ligne Roset), real wear ("one chair has a small repair," "one small chip on the back corner"). ✓
2. **Necessity.** Each listing carries enough to read it as real and decide if you want to click. No filler descriptions. ✓
3. **Voice.** Matches the listing card samples in `voice-and-copy.md` — short clauses, honest about flaws, no marketing-speak, no exclamation marks. ✓
4. **Action.** Each listing closes with a clear pickup neighborhood and any conditions (delivery, this-week-only). The reader knows what to do next. ✓
5. **Honesty.** Flaws are named (chip, scratch, fading edge, loose drawer pull, repaired chair). Reissues are labeled as reissues, not originals. No overclaim. ✓
