# Manhattanite — Services and perks seed listings v1

Drafted 2026-08-27 to fix the thing George caught on the live site: browse reads as a
rental website. The cause is the mix — 12 of 20 listings are apartments, and **Services
and "Everything else" are both at zero**. No design change fixes that. Listings do.

These 14 fill the two empty categories, and they cover the two pillars the product has
never demonstrated: **community services** (vetted people you can hire) and **access**
(what Manhattanites can extend to each other). Same conventions as the existing seed
docs: `is_example = true`, published, American spelling, members on first names only,
sponsor = George during seed, honest about limits.

---

## REVISED RECOMMENDATION (George, 2026-08-27) — seed FOUR of these, not fourteen

The first version of this document recommended adding all 14. **That was wrong**, and
George caught it. Fourteen new example listings would make the site roughly 70% invented,
every one of them sponsored by George and tagged Example — further from "a stranger would
believe it's real", not closer. It also cuts against the seeding approach already decided
with Cole: friends listing as a favour, taste as the product, real beats volume.

**The mix problem does not need new listings. It needs fewer apartments.**

| | Apartments | Furniture | Services | Everything else | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Today | 12 (60%) | 8 | 0 | 0 | 20 |
| Cut 4 apartments, add 4 | **8 (40%)** | 8 | 2 | 2 | **20** |

Cutting four seeded apartments invents nothing and does most of the work. Four additions
then fill both empty categories and demonstrate all three price states. **The network stays
exactly as small as it is now**, which is the point of it.

**The four to seed:** SV1 or SV2 (a service with a price), SV4 or SV6 (a service quoted on
request — blank price), EE2 (free at $0), EE5 (a perk with no price). Final pick is George's.

**The other ten are a bench, not a backlog.** Several are more useful as scripts for real
asks than as fabrications — **EE5 is word-for-word the conversation the First-Five-Offerings
playbook already tells George to go and have with a neighborhood business.** One real perk
from one real shop is worth more than ten invented ones, and it is the evidence the discount
pillar actually needs.

**Still open:** no real business is named anywhere in this draft, on purpose. An example
listing that names a real hotel or coffee shop invents a relationship that does not exist
and attributes an endorsement no business gave — on a product whose entire pitch is that a
name attached to a listing means something. Every business here is described, not named.
Real names go in only where George has the real relationship.

## What these demonstrate that the current seed cannot

**Three different kinds of "no number", and they are not the same thing.** The blank-price
change shipped today makes this postable for the first time:

- **A price** — an hourly rate, a monthly fee. Ordinary.
- **`$0`, meaning free** — the tickets, the twenty minutes. A real, deliberate price.
- **No price at all** — a quote, a members' rate, a perk. Blank, renders as nothing.

If the seed only ever shows priced listings, the feature we just built is invisible.

## Photos

Twelve of these fourteen are photograph-free, and that is correct rather than a gap — a
tailor, a dog walker and a members' rate have nothing to show, and forcing a stock image
onto them would be the first dishonest thing on the site. Only **EE6** wants a photograph
(a storage cage) and it is the one most easily skipped.

This also means the batch needs **no new image sourcing**, so it can be seeded the moment
George approves the copy.

## To seed these

Do NOT hand-write rows. `scripts/seed-example-listings.ts` is the generator — it creates the
four example members through the real approval path, uploads photos, and is idempotent with
a working `--unseed`. Extend its `LISTINGS` array from this document and re-run
`npm run seed:examples`. Requires the `0027` migration, which was applied to production on
2026-08-27, or every blank-price row here will be rejected by the database.
