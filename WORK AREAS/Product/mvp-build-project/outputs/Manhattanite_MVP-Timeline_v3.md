# Manhattanite — Launch Timeline (v3, the recut)

**Re-anchored:** 2026-08-31 · **Replaces:** the tail of v2 (Phases 6–8, which ran out on 30 August) · **Covers:** 1 September – 4 October 2026

v2 got the build finished. It did not get the launch scheduled, and its last week expired yesterday with nobody invited. This recut is one page: five weeks, one must-hit each, ending in a decision rather than another deadline.

---

## Where the build actually is

**Finished and live on manhattanite.com.** Sign up, apply, approve, post, browse, contact, edit, take down, and a full admin console. The trust wall is proven at the database, not just in the interface — 67 of 67 checks, run against production, not a copy. The site is four to five times faster than it was a week ago.

**Nothing on the build side is blocking a launch.** That is the change v2 never got to record, and it is why this recut is not an apology. The product is finished enough to invite people to. What is missing is people.

---

## What changed, honestly

August went to work that was not in v2 at all: the Classifieds design system became the site, which meant migrating every screen onto it, plus an admin console, two database migrations and a speed pass. That was the right call and it cost about three weeks. Phases 6 and 7 were partly overtaken by it — some of the hardening happened, the launch prep did not.

**The rule of slippage is unchanged:** if a week slips, scope comes out of the second invite wave. Never out of the checks in Week 1. Inviting real people onto a site you have not walked yourself is the one mistake here that costs trust rather than time.

---

## Week 1 · Tue 1 – Sun 6 September — Launch prep

**Must-hit: you have walked every screen a first invitee will touch, and the invitation path has been tested end to end with a real email address.**

- **Test the invitation path on yourself.** Send an invite to a personal address, open the link, set a password, land inside. This path has never been used by a real person, and it is the first thing a first member does. If one link in that chain is broken, the launch is broken.
- **Confirm `Test - Ignore` is off the site.** Unresolved since the 27th; the button that removes it now works.
- **Secure the first two or three real offerings.** Cole, Cody, the mover, the shop perk. Not the music lessons — your call, and the right one: the opening listings set the standard people judge the network by. A wave-one member should land on at least a couple of real things, not only labelled examples.
- **Write the community guidelines.** Last unwritten piece of Phase 7. Terms and Privacy are live and now say "vouching" throughout.
- **Decide hello@ vs info@** before it appears in a hundred invitations.
- **Wire error reporting.** Ten days flagged, and it matters the moment strangers are on the site: without it, you find out something broke when a member tells you, not when it happens.

## Week 2 · Mon 7 – Sun 13 September — The first wave

**Must-hit: 10 invitations sent to real people.**

- Labor Day is Monday the 7th. Send from Tuesday.
- Ten, not twenty-five. Ten is enough to learn from and small enough that you can answer every one of them personally in the same week.
- **Fold the five friend interviews into this wave rather than running them separately.** The people you invite are the people you wanted to interview. One conversation each: what they made of it, what they would list, what they would never list. That closes an open task and gets you better answers than a scheduled interview would, because they have just used the thing.
- Watch the queue daily. Approve by hand. Every application gets a reply.

## Week 3 · Mon 14 – Sun 20 September — Respond

**Must-hit: every wave-one invitation has an answer, and the first real member-to-member contact has happened.**

- **NANM, Friday 18 to Sunday 20 — no Manhattanite work those three days.** Both this weekend and the next are fully committed.
- Fix what wave one trips over. No new features.
- The measure of a soft launch is not how many were invited. It is whether two members who did not know each other used the product to reach one another. Until that happens once, the thing is unproven.

## Week 4 · Mon 21 – Sun 27 September — The second wave, conditionally

**Must-hit: 10 to 15 more invitations — but only if wave one produced real listings.**

- **NANM again, Friday 25 to Sunday 27.**
- If wave one produced accounts but no listings, do not send wave two. Send the same ten another reason to come back instead. Inviting more people into an empty network makes it look emptier, twice as fast.

## Week 5 · Mon 28 September – Sun 4 October — Assess

**Must-hit: a written read on whether the product holds, and a decision on what comes next.**

Three questions, answered from what actually happened rather than from what you hoped:

1. Does the loop work — invite, join, post, contact — with people who are not you?
2. What did every one of them trip over? That list is the v1.5 spec.
3. Do the parked questions reopen now, with evidence? The audience decision, the three-pillar framing, the one-line pitch, the professional designer, the Cole partnership. All five were deliberately deferred until real people had used it. This is that moment.

---

## Not in scope, on purpose

- **No public launch.** Nothing about this is announced.
- **No new features** between now and 4 October. The one exception is a fix for something a real member hits.
- **Image weight** — every listing photo is around 900kb to fill a card 230 pixels wide. It is the biggest remaining technical lever and it is not urgent enough to delay a launch. Revisit in v1.5, most likely by resizing photos as they are uploaded.
- **Analytics.** Privacy says you do not run any, which is currently true and is a fine thing to be true during a soft launch of ten people.

---

## Reality checks

- **One must-hit a week.** Hit only that and this still works.
- **Two of the five weekends are gone to the band.** The plan assumes that; it is not slippage when it happens.
- **The 90-minute weekday block is the unit of work.** Everything above is sized for it. Week 1 is roughly four blocks.
- **The failure mode to watch is not slipping a week — it is inviting nobody while the site gets a little better.** The build is finished. More polish is now avoidance.
