# Project brief: Monetization

## What is this project?

Deciding when Manhattanite starts charging to post, what it charges for, how much, and what has to be true before the first dollar is taken.

## Goal

A confirmed price list and a switch-on trigger George is happy to commit to, plus the build and legal work that has to land before payments go live.

## Audience

Internal. Feeds the Legal work area (entity formation) and the Product build (Stripe).

## Key constraints

Already decided and not to be reopened without cause:

- **Pay-per-post only.** No paid membership tiers, no business accounts, no broker packages. Membership free forever. (2026-05-17, confirmed)
- **Free to browse, free to sponsor, free to be a member.** (2026-05-16)
- **No ads on the site.** Paid acquisition off the table while seeding; ads as a revenue line remain out permanently. (2026-07-13)
- **No entity registration until money is about to change hands** — the first dollar is itself a trigger. (2026-07-02, George)
- Every listing is manually reviewed by George. Moderation throughput, not money, is the ceiling on this business.
