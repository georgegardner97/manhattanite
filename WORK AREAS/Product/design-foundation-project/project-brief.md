# Project brief: Design foundation

## What is this project?

Taking Manhattanite's design and branding from "okay" to amazing — chic, eye-catching, elegant, very professional. This is the styling pass that was deliberately deferred during the MVP build (the "Design Foundation slot" flagged in CLAUDE.md), now activated as its own project.

## Goal

Every public screen reads as an identity, not a styled template. The deferred brand decisions (wordmark, final palette, serif) get made in context on real screens. A member screenshotting any page onto Instagram should feel proud of the association — the brand guide's own bar.

## Sequencing decision (2026-07-16)

**Product screens first, brand lock second.** George's call, matching the brand guide's own instruction: decide on a screen, not a swatch. Full sequence and method in `outputs/Manhattanite_Design-Foundation-Plan_v1.md`.

## Working method

- **Mobbin** — reference collection (George's account, "Manhattanite" collection)
- **Claude Design (claude.ai/design)** — mockups and the component library, kept in sync with the codebase
- **The repo** — Claude implements; George decides, never codes

One session = one output = one decision. Decisions log to `memory.md` here.

## Key constraints

- No new features. Styling and identity only.
- Doesn't block, and isn't blocked by, the member-acquisition or legal tracks.
- American spelling in all product copy (brand rule); the brand guide's do/don't table is the quality gate.
- Optional spends only: Mobbin Pro (if the free cap bites) and a serif licence (if a paid font clearly wins on screen).
