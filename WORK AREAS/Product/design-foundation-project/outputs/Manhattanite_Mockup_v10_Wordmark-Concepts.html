Three ways to sign the name.

Same pages, same palette — only the signature changes. Each concept is shown in the four places it has to live: the dark hero, the light product nav, the browser tab, and the card that appears when someone texts a manhattanite.com link.

Concept A
The incumbent

Roman serif, italic “ite” — what’s live today, kept deliberately. The stress lands on the ending: a Manhattan­ite is a person, and the italic says so.

How to choose.

Squint. The one that still feels like the pitch deck, the dark landing, and the paper inside — at 16 pixels and at 34 — is the wordmark. The favicon and share card ship in the same session as the decision.
