## Week 1 (w/c 6 July) — build the list, start the machine

### 1. The list (do this first — one sitting, 45 minutes)

Brain-dump 30 names into `Founding-Members_List_v1.md`. Don't filter while dumping; filter after. Sources, in playbook order of yield:

- Friends and acquaintances who fit (first-degree)
- People those friends would vouch for (second-degree — you'll ask them later)
- Faces from specific scenes: Casa Magazines regulars, neighbourhood spots' staff and owners, design studios, indie media people
- The people already running the informal version — whoever posts apartment leads in your WhatsApp groups

Then tag each name against the composition target: **Anna** (mover/young professional), **Max** (established local), **Lila** (newcomer). Target is roughly 8/8/4 for the final 20, so the list of 30 should skew similar.

### 2. Fair-housing checklist (15 minutes, Claude drafts)

A short checklist added to the moderation pass for apartment listings: no stated preferences on family status, source of income, national origin, or any protected class; describe the apartment, not the ideal tenant. This is the one legal item that survives the entity deferral.

### 3. Materials (Claude drafts, George edits)

- **Outreach email/text template** — personal, in voice-and-copy style. Two sentences of what it is, one specific reason they're wanted, ask for a coffee or a 10-minute walkthrough. Not a pitch deck.
- **30-second walkthrough** — screen recording of the live site, no narration. Record once the seed listings are in (see 4).

### 4. Seed listings — ~~load them~~ DONE (verified on prod 2026-07-02)

The example listings are live with photos: guest teaser shows 6, landing image band renders, all clearly labelled "Example" per spec. The site looks alive. Remaining niggles only: hard-delete the archived QA-test listing, and give the full logged-in catalogue a once-over for quality before the first coffee demo.

### 5. First conversations (3 this week)

Three coffees or calls with the easiest first-degree names. Use the playbook script: demo, then the two questions — *"Would you use this?"* and *"Who are the three Manhattanites you'd vouch for?"* Log answers (especially the vouched names) in the tracker.

## The recurring routine (from week 3)

The playbook offers two cadences. Given ADHD, the playbook itself suggests the collapsed version. **Recommendation: take the 2-day version.**

| Day | Block |
|---|---|
| **Monday** | Outreach batch: 10 messages, tracker update, application review |
| **Thursday** | People: 2–3 meets/calls, follow-ups, one listing seeded, quick log entry |

Daily (5 min, phone-friendly): check for new applications. Response time is a brand signal — target under 48 hours even during seed.

## What "done" looks like for the seed phase

- 20 members, all using the site at least weekly
- Real listings outnumber example listings
- A bench of vouched-for names ready for Cohort 1

## Anti-patterns (from the playbook — reread when tempted)

- No LinkedIn "I'm building something" post
- No press, no waitlist page
- No more than 5 invites in any week
- No marginal-fit onboarding to keep the funnel moving
