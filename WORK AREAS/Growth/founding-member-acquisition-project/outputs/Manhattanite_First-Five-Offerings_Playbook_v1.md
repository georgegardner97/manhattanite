# The first five offerings — micro-step playbook (v1)

**Dated 2026-08-13.** The 5 example offerings from the strategy session (§7), broken into steps small enough that none takes more than ~15 minutes. This is bigger than a coaching exercise: these five people become the **first real members**, and their listings the **first real (non-EXAMPLE) content** on the network. This is the grassroots wave, in miniature.

## The pipeline every offering runs through (learn it once)

Every offering follows the same six beats — the wall stays absolute (poster becomes a member first, no exceptions):

**Ask → Account (they sign up at manhattanite.com) → Apply (they submit /apply, ~3 min) → Approve (you: `npm run approve -- <app-id>` from your Mac; they get the "You're in." email) → List (they post; you help with photos/copy) → Moderate (you approve the listing; it goes live).**

Your only prep before any ask: nothing. The site is live, the emails work, the approval CLI works. The machine is built — these five are the first real cargo.

**One system note (pillar 2 vs pillar 3 vs pillar 1):** services list under the "Service" type (supported since migration 0019), goods under furniture/other. The **discount pillar has no native mechanic yet** — offering #4 below deliberately tests it as a plain listing ("Members: mention Manhattanite for X") before any build work. If it works, *that's* the evidence for a v1.5 feature; don't build first.

**Recommended order: 5 → 3 → 1 → 2 → 4** (zero-ask first, warmest ask second, hardest mechanic last).

## Offering 5 — Music lessons, by George *(do first: no ask needed, you're already a member)*

The founder as proof-of-concept, and it markets your Income project for free.

1. ☐ **(10 min)** Pull the one-page site copy from `WORK AREAS/Income/music-teaching-project/outputs/` and cut it to listing length: what you teach (bass/guitar 1:1, small-group songwriting), for whom, where, from-price. Voice rules apply — concrete, no hype.
2. ☐ **(10 min)** Photos per the new photo rules: 2–3 daylight shots — your instrument corner at home, a detail (amp, notebook of songs). No stock, no filters.
3. ☐ **(5 min)** Post it: /listings/new → type "Service" → submit for review.
4. ☐ **(2 min)** Approve it in moderation. **The network now has its first real listing.**

## Offering 3 — Cole's first listings *(warmest ask — she volunteered)*

She said, unprompted, she can "pretty immediately" think of things she'd list. Take her up on it — it doubles as the partnership's first working session.

1. ☐ **(5 min)** Text her — script: *"You mentioned you could immediately think of things you'd list. Want to be the first real member? Ten minutes: you make an account, apply, I approve you, we list one thing. Then you've seen the whole machine you'd be marketing."*
2. ☐ **(0 min, hers)** She creates an account + submits /apply. (Side benefit: the first-ever human eyes on the dark /apply form — the one screen never visually verified.)
3. ☐ **(2 min)** Approve her: `npm run approve -- <app-id>` (ping email lands in info@ with the command ready).
4. ☐ **(15 min, together)** Help her post her first item — you do photo guidance live (daylight, whole piece + one honest detail). Approve it.
5. ☐ **(5 min)** Log what confused her — every stumble is onboarding gold — and add her 3 vouched names to the bench.

## Offering 1 — Cody the dog walker *(the trust-product demo)*

A vouched dog walker is the purest "community-based service" there is, and he's a neighborhood node who sees everyone.

1. ☐ **(2 min)** Next time you see him (or text): *"I've built a members-only site for the neighborhood — I want you on it as one of the first. People find you by word of mouth anyway; this is word of mouth, written down. I'll set you up in ten minutes."*
2. ☐ **(10 min, together on his phone)** Account + /apply. If forms aren't his thing, you type while he talks — his words, though (moderation never rewrites; posters own their copy).
3. ☐ **(2 min)** Approve him.
4. ☐ **(10 min)** His listing: what he does, which blocks, since when, from-price. One photo of him with a dog he walks (owner's OK first — photo rules).
5. ☐ **(5 min)** Ask him the two questions — a dog walker's 3 vouched names will be *very* good bench material.

## Offering 2 — The vetted mover / handyman *(need to pick the person first)*

1. ☐ **(5 min, tonight)** Name the person: scroll your phone — who moved you, fixed something, or got passed around your group chats with "he's great"? Write ONE name down. (If nobody surfaces, ask Beau or Cole: "who's your guy?" — that's the product working before the product.)
2. ☐ **(2 min)** Same ask script as Cody, adapted.
3. ☐ **(15 min)** Same pipeline: account → apply → approve → listing (their trade, their patch, from-price, one real job photo).
4. ☐ **(5 min)** Two questions, bench the names.

## Offering 4 — One neighborhood-business perk *(hardest, do last, keep it tiny)*

Testing the discounts pillar with one conversation, zero build.

1. ☐ **(5 min)** Pick the ONE East Village spot where they know your name — coffee, framing, dry cleaning. Known face beats best deal.
2. ☐ **(5 min, in person)** The ask, kept micro: *"I run a small members-only site for the neighborhood — a few dozen vetted locals, growing. Would you do a small perk for members — 10% off, or a free coffee with a bag of beans? I'll send you customers; costs you nothing until someone walks in."*
3. ☐ **(10 min)** If yes: post it yourself as the member ("Other" type) — *"[Shop], [street]. Mention Manhattanite: [perk]. One of ours."* The owner doesn't need an account for the pilot — you're vouching for the perk, they're not transacting on the platform.
4. ☐ **(later)** After 2–3 members exist, ask the owner: "anyone come in?" That answer is the entire pillar-1 validation.
