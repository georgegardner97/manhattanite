# Project brief: Founding member acquisition

## What is this project?

Getting the first 20 members onto Manhattanite (the Seed phase of `COMPANY/gtm-playbook.md`), then converting them into the sponsor engine for members 20–80. Founder-led, private on purpose.

## Goal

20 high-fit members, each using the site at least once a week, with real listings starting to outnumber example listings. That's the trigger to Phase 2 (Cohort 1).

Composition target (playbook default, still open to revision): ~8 movers/young professionals, ~8 established locals, ~4 newcomers. Mostly Manhattan.

## Audience

Internal — George's working project. The people being recruited: socially networked, taste-conscious, Manhattan-resident, 25–40, professionally established, willing to vouch. Already sharing listings/intel informally in WhatsApp groups or Instagram stories.

## Key constraints

- **Sequencing decision (2026-07-02, revised same day): full go.** Outreach, coffees, list-building, seed listings, and member approvals all start immediately. **Entity formation deferred** until a trigger: first dollar taken, ~50+ members, or members George doesn't personally know — whichever first. The surviving guardrail: a fair-housing checklist in the moderation pass for apartment listings (exposure exists regardless of money or entity).
- No paid ads, ever. No press, no waitlist page, no LinkedIn announcement during seed.
- Max ~5 invites per week — shape of growth matters more than speed.
- Never onboard a marginal fit to keep the funnel going. This is the named failure mode.
- Playbook assumptions still unconfirmed: 8/8/4 split, founder-routine cadence (weekly vs 2-day-a-week), printed cards, Instagram-first vs no social.
