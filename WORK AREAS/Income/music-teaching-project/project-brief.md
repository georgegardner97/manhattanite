# Project brief — music teaching

**Created:** 2026-08-01
**Work area:** Income (new area, created for non-Manhattanite income work)
**Status:** Active — week-one actions drafted

## Goal

Build a part-time income stream teaching music to children in NYC: 1:1 bass/guitar lessons as the entry offer, songwriting/creativity workshops as the differentiator. Target $2,500–4,000/month at full ramp (realistically by month 4–6), without touching the Manhattanite 9–4 weekday block.

## Why this

- Kids' teaching hours (weekdays 3:30–7pm, weekend mornings) sit outside the Manhattanite deep-work block
- NYC direct in-home rates ($80–150/hr) make the maths work at 6–10 hrs/week
- Different brain from building the app, so it doesn't drain the founder tank
- Songwriting angle uses George's actual practice and is the word-of-mouth story

## Strategy

Agencies first for students and teaching reps (pay $24–59/hr), private roster built in parallel (2–3x the rate). Group songwriting labs are the leverage play. Online lessons via Lessonface keep it partly portable for away-months.

## Open questions

- Guitar vs bass-and-songwriting-only positioning (George is a bassist; beginner guitar likely fine but needs honest prep)
- Pricing (bracketed in one-pager; set after first agency interviews)
- Whether harmonica hour floats on teaching days
