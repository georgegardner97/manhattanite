## Version A: listserv / school newsletter post

**Subject: Guitar & bass lessons in the East Village. Kids write their own songs.**

Hi all, I'm George, a working musician (bass, guitar, songwriting) based in the East Village, and I've opened up a few weekly slots for kids' lessons this fall.

My approach is simple: kids stick with music when they make something of their own. So alongside the fundamentals, every student works toward writing and recording a real song. Their melody, their words, their name on it. It does more for practice motivation than any sticker chart.

What I offer:

- **1:1 guitar or bass lessons** (ages 6+), at your home or mine. Beginners welcome.
- **Songwriting lab** (ages 8 to 14): small groups of up to 6, one Saturday session, everyone leaves with a finished song.

Weekday slots are 3:30 to 7pm, with some weekend times too. References available, and I'm happy to do a free 20-minute meet-and-greet so your kid can decide if we click.

Reach me at [email] or [phone]. George
