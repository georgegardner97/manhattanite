## The problem

Most kids' music lessons follow the same arc: excitement, then scales, then dread, then quitting. The instrument goes under the bed by spring.

It's rarely the kid's fault, and it's not really the teacher's either. It's the format. When the only goal is "get better at exercises," there's nothing to show anyone, and kids run on making things, not on drills.

## How I teach

We learn the fundamentals through songs your kid already loves, and from early on we point everything at one goal: writing their own song. Their melody, their words. When it's ready, we record it properly, and they get the track.

A kid who has written one song practices differently. They're not doing homework anymore. They're working on their thing.
