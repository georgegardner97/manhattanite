## What Manhattanite is

Manhattanite is a trust-based, invite-only online classifieds platform for Manhattan residents — loosely modeled on France's Gens de Confiance, but for New York. Members browse and post local listings (launching with two categories: **apartments** and **furniture**). The platform connects members; it is not a party to any transaction between them.

Access is two-tier: a free account can browse and apply; full membership (post, contact) requires approval. There is no in-platform payment or messaging at launch — contact happens via a form that triggers an email. Monetization (a pay-per-post fee) is planned for a later version, not at launch.

The product is already built and running in production with a small number of test listings. **No company has been formed yet** — that's the first thing I need to fix.

## Founder situation

- Solo founder, self-funded. Open to bringing on a partner or first hire later, but no investment round or formal equity grants planned in the near term.
- Based in and resident in New York.
- Non-technical founder; please explain options in plain terms.

## Current legal posture (seed phase)

To keep early risk low, the platform is currently: not publicly advertised, not processing any payments, not showing listings to non-account-holders, and not accepting paid promotion.

## What I'd like a quote for

I'm looking for a small, defined engagement covering the items below. Please flag anything you'd sequence differently, anything I've missed, and where you'd want a specialist (e.g., NYC housing law).

**1. Entity formation.** Confirm the right structure for my situation — my working assumption is a **New York LLC**, with a Delaware C-Corp only if you think likely fundraising or equity grants justify it. Then form the entity, including the operating agreement, EIN, the NY publication requirement, and the new beneficial-ownership filing.

**2. Terms of Service + Privacy Policy.** Draft (or review templates for) both, tailored to a marketplace. Priorities: clearly establishing that Manhattanite is not a party to member transactions; acceptable-use and listing standards; account termination; NY SHIELD Act and general privacy compliance; and handling of users from outside NY (CA/EU).

**3. Fair-housing review for apartment listings.** This is my biggest concern. I'd like guidance on what NYC and federal fair-housing law require of a platform that publishes apartment listings, and sign-off on my plan to mitigate it (structured listing fields, a pre-publication review checklist, visible listing guidelines, and a complaint mechanism). I want this settled before any member other than me posts an apartment.
