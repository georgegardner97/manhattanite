# Project Brief — Company Formation & Pre-Launch Legal

**Work area:** Legal
**Created:** 2026-06-09
**Owner:** George

## Goal
Get Manhattanite legally stood up for launch: form the company, get core policies live, and de-risk apartment listings — without over-spending at the seed stage.

## Context
George has a live MVP (real listings in prod) but no registered company. Self-funded, solo founder, open to bringing someone on later, NY-based/resident. Strategic legal map already exists in `COMPANY/legal-and-policy.md`; this project turns it into concrete action.

## The three "do now" items
1. Form the entity — recommended NY LLC (Delaware C-Corp only if raising/equity within ~12–18 months). Watch the Manhattan LLC publication cost (~$1,200–2,000).
2. Terms of Service + Privacy Policy live before real users sign up. Key clause: platform is not a party to member transactions.
3. Fair-housing guardrails on apartment listings before any non-George apartment post goes live (biggest risk; Section 230 doesn't reliably shield housing).

## Parked until trigger
Sales tax (at monetization), payments/banking (v2), trademark (at traction; free USPTO search now), insurance (at revenue/scale), employment paperwork (at first hire).

## Immediate next action
Book a paid scoping call with a NY startup attorney with marketplace experience. Decide LLC vs C-Corp and have them form the entity + draft/review Terms + Privacy.
